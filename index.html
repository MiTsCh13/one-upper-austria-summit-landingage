Many strengths. One shared future.
More opportunities.

Upper Austria combines successful startups, strong research institutions and an innovative industry with global market leaders, market access and unlocked growth potential.

One Upper Austria gives the whole ecosystem a platform to showcase our strengths and possibilities. By working together, we build an annual flagship event that creates lasting opportunities and strengthens Upper Austria's international profile as a place where innovation happens.

Let's open our networks to one another and combine the strengths of our ecosystem to establish Upper Austria as a driving force for innovation in Europe.

Venue

Tabakfabrik Linz

No other place stands for transformation, creativity and new ways of doing business like the Tabakfabrik. As the heart of Upper Austria's startup scene, it's the perfect home – spatially and symbolically – for a summit about change, collaboration and the future.

Equal partners. Shared responsibility. A common goal.

Organised by the ecosystem – for the ecosystem

Partners co-create the programme and agenda through the organising committee, contribute according to their capabilities and actively engage their communities.

By pooling resources, expertise, infrastructure, people and funding, we deliver a high-quality event while keeping costs lean. The summit is planned on a break-even basis, with free community ticket allocations linked to the agreed financial and in-kind contributions of all partners. Together, we make this a collective event.
